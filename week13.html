November 15 - November 23, 2025: Settling in Copenhagen

  • Week of Nov 16 – Nov 23
  • Nov 16: Played some lovely frisbee in the sunset (at 4pm :(( ). These early sunsets make me a little sad given all the energy that I still want to experience. Yes, the night is the night, but it’s a lot harder to get out of my dorm when the sun has set, and my motivation to leave my room is far lower than when the sun is out. I’m a nut about getting sun and seeing it.
  • Nov 17: Got a haircut on Nørrebrogade for 150 DKK. First time touching cash because that’s how I paid the guy. The day and week of the haircut I always feel iffy, but now I like it and it has grown in well, so I’m happy with it.
  • Nov 18: Sprints in Fælledparken. Met a dog. Had a good time doing sprints and restarting with Woody’s workouts.
  • Nov 19: Pickup frisbee with DIS frisbee pals and Peri’s birthday. CopenHot and spent time with Adina, who also goes to Wellesley! In retrospect, this is definitely when I first got sick because the sauna opened my sinuses and burned, then the cold plunge. Still, it was lovely to spend time with friends and a new friend, and I enjoyed the experience despite the overwhelming temperature changes, especially since it was raining.
  • Nov 20: Dinner with Chelsea at Hello Pasta, which was so tasty, then we walked around Nyhavn. I’m so happy we’re friends and it’s always so easy to talk with her. We talked about wanting to eat through Copenhagen in our last few weeks and how food is a way we consume culture that feels more real than museums. Also, it SNOWED!!!
  • Nov 21: Met my visiting host family for the first time! It’s a DIS program where students meet with a family without living with them. Mine were a mother and daughter named Louise and Caylee. We had Christmas dinner (duck and caramelized potatoes). It was quite tasty, and I enjoyed meeting them. I taught the daughter a hand-clapping game my campers loved.
  • Nov 22: Nothing and yet something. Juno the Bakery (worth it!). They had a whole operation and the line wasn’t too long—or rather, it moved, and I wasn’t in a rush. Then Køge, the end of the A S-tog, which was nice.
  • Nov 23: Walked around Copenhagen! Lovely time and a good frisbee practice.
